# Guest and owner experience

The owner chooses Whole shelf, Open only or a flight. Eligible bottles have Opened or Unopened status. The default is Whole shelf, a 12-second rotation, ratings on, photos on and estimated values off. Rotation also supports 8 and 30 seconds. The owner previews the bottle facts and images and can enlarge the photos to inspect their labels and backgrounds. A failed preview image blocks startup. Turning off photos yields silhouettes.

Guests see the bottle name, brand, type, proof, age, store-pick flag, status and fill when available. Optional stars and individual secondary-market estimates appear only when enabled. The response excludes owner or bottle identifiers, email, plan, quantity, owner lists, free-text notes and tags, purchase price, MSRP, collection valuation, storage locations and original media URLs. Guests see the actual pixels the owner approved. Free-text bottle names and information visible in those images are part of the preview.

Showcase presents one bottle with six thumbnail positions, manual navigation and Pause/Resume. Selecting a bottle pauses rotation. Browse shows ten bottles per page at full HD and six at a 1024-pixel tablet width. Categories include Everything, Bourbon, Rye, Store picks, Open to pour and Five stars. Hiding ratings also removes Five stars. Selecting a tile opens facts and its photographs. Close preserves the shelf page and filter. Back to showcase returns to the selected bottle. Sixty seconds without browse activity returns to showcase. Reduced motion starts and returns paused.

The app asks for a screen wake lock where available. The guest can enter full screen using the header control. Exiting browser full screen retains guest mode. Leaving guest mode requires the per-display PIN or fresh authentication as the owner who started it. Either path ends at sign-in. Sessions expire eight hours after activation regardless of guest activity.

The approved decisions replace the handoff’s optional unlocked exit, broad “prices and values” switch and sample collector name. Missing photos use a silhouette. Finished, archived and unknown records stay outside the selected shelf.
