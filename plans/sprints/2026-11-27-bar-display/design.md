# Guest and owner experience

The owner chooses Whole shelf, Open only or a flight. Eligible bottles have Opened or Unopened status. The default is Whole shelf, a 12-second rotation, ratings on, photos on and estimated values off. Rotation also supports 8 and 30 seconds. The owner previews the bottle facts and images and can enlarge the photos to inspect their labels and backgrounds. A failed preview image blocks startup. Turning off photos yields silhouettes.

Setup offers per-field exclusions under **Choose bottle details**. Guests can see recorded bottle, distilling, finish, barrel, release and store-pick details, including personal tags, unless the owner excludes them. Estimated value remains opt-in. Personal and tasting notes, purchase price, MSRP, quantity, storage location, account data, internal IDs and original media URLs stay private. The owner preview shows the projected values and the actual photo pixels. Text or images the owner shares may contain information visible on the label.

Tapping the featured bottle pauses rotation. **Bottle details** opens the fact card, and **Enlarge photo** opens the fitted photo. Either action pauses the selected bottle. Close or Escape returns to the opener. A single photo uses a bottom zoom toolbar. Multiple photos have image-only thumbnail buttons, a check on the selected photo, accessible names and approved facts.

The showcase fits the complete photograph at its original proportions on the page background. Previous and Next sit beside the shelf position. Guests can pause or resume rotation and use Browse shelf to jump to another bottle. Selecting a bottle pauses rotation. Browse shows ten bottles per page at full HD and six at a 1024-pixel tablet width. Categories include Everything, Bourbon, Rye, Store picks, Open to pour and Five stars. Hiding ratings also removes Five stars. Selecting a tile opens facts and its photographs. Close preserves the shelf page and filter. Back to showcase returns to the selected bottle. Sixty seconds without browse activity returns to showcase. Reduced motion starts and returns paused.

The app asks for a screen wake lock where available. The guest can enter full screen using the header control. Exiting browser full screen retains guest mode. Leaving guest mode requires the per-display PIN or fresh authentication as the owner who started it. Either path ends at sign-in. Sessions expire eight hours after activation regardless of guest activity.

The approved decisions replace the handoff’s optional unlocked exit, broad “prices and values” switch and sample collector name. Missing photos use a silhouette. Finished, archived and unknown records stay outside the selected shelf.
