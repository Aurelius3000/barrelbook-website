# Acceptance Criteria

- `/terms` presents the supplied July 18, 2026 Terms of Service.
- `/privacy` presents the supplied July 18, 2026 Privacy Policy.
- `/terms/2025-09-17` preserves the prior Terms of Service.
- `/privacy/2025-09-17` preserves the prior Privacy Policy.
- Each current page links to its matching prior version.
- Each archived page is clearly identified and links back to the current version.
- Archived pages are excluded from search indexing.
- Footer and sitemap links remain canonical.
- Desktop and mobile layouts remain readable.
- `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, and `npm run build` pass or any pre-existing warnings are documented.
