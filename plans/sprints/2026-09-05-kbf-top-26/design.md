# Design direction

Selected direction: Drop Schedule / collector planner.

Copy rule from Pete: short sentences, active voice, plain words, fewer words, no em dashes.
Use Confirmed, Reported, and Watch for KBF status. Show source links next to known facts.
Use the site's Geist font for body text and Georgia for the prototype's serif headings.
Reuse the existing logo. Build the shared look with Tailwind.

The visual language uses BarrelBook's near-black surfaces, charcoal dividers, white type, and restrained burnt-orange emphasis. List numbers, release status, and specs are easy to scan. The page does not imply that each bottle has a known sale time. Detail pages share the same records and show sources and updates.

The build uses the existing BarrelBook logo. It uses no bottle photos. A later photo needs rights and an exact bottle match.

## Browser review

The built pages keep the approved dark layout, serif headings, numbered rows, and side filters. List numbers follow the master order, not a quality rank. Tier labels and the tier filter are gone.
Phone filters fold into a keyboard-friendly panel. Source labels sit beside each known spec.
Unknown specs show TBD. Reported days keep their label even when bottle specs have a brand source.

Six day tabs cover all bottles, Thursday through Sunday, and Day TBD. Thursday is backed by the new Phifer Pavitt source, not its social slot. The open pick has a dashed card below the unfiltered list. It has no bottle link. Parked pages show a clear banner and a link to current picks.

Desktop and phone screenshots were reviewed. The index has no sideways scroll at 320, 390, 768, 1024, or 1440 pixels. All 29 detail pages pass at 320, 390, and 1440 pixels. See `evidence.md` for screenshot paths.
