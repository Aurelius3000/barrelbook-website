- The same brand release lists bottle sales at the Whiskey House chalet Sept. 10 to 13, while stock lasts. Friday and Saturday tasting hours do not establish sale hours. Price, KBF count, and exact sale hours stay TBD.
- [Bourbon & Banter's guide](https://www.bourbonbanter.com/the-ultimate-insiders-guide-to-the-2026-kentucky-bourbon-festival/) lists Jackson Purchase's KBF cask-strength single barrel. The record is Reported. Age, numeric proof, price, count, day, time, and place stay TBD.
- The guide identifies the WhistlePig 12-year KBF single barrel as the Vault team's pick. Its three cask types match the retained record. The public name now includes Vault; the record stays Reported.
- The guide still does not settle King of Kentucky's release year or bottle sales. Do not infer the year from its URL or borrow specs from another release.
- Retained facts keep their earlier check dates unless this pass checked them. The roster update date is separate from a full source refresh.

## First-pass states
