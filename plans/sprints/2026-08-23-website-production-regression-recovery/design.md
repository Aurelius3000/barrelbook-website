# Design Notes

The homepage warning appears immediately below the fixed header. It uses BarrelBook's existing dark brown and amber support treatment and remains a single, calm path to `/support`.

Approved copy:

- `Having trouble signing in to BarrelBook or seeing your collection?`
- `Get help with BarrelBook 1.7.3 →`

The warning must not replace or visually alter the App Store rating, App Store badge, or Android early-access CTA. The support route retains the existing screenshot-led recovery instructions and safe warning to contact Support before deleting the app when changes may not have synced.
